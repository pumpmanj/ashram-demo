Services

Care that fits your situation

Tap a service to see what it is, who it helps, and what's included.

Skilled NursingA nurse visits your home

What it is

A licensed nurse visits on a schedule to check your health, manage medications, and keep your doctor informed.

Who it helps

People recovering from a hospital stay or surgery, or living with heart, lung, diabetes, or other ongoing conditions.

What's included

  • Health assessments and vital signs
  • Medication management
  • Managing chronic conditions
  • Recovery after hospital or surgery
  • Updates to your doctor
  • Teaching for you and your family
Private Duty NursingLonger nursing visits for complex needs

What it is

Extended nursing care at home for patients who need more hours of skilled support.

Who it helps

Adults or children with complex or ongoing medical needs who need a nurse for longer periods.

What's included

  • Extended nursing shifts at home
  • Ongoing clinical monitoring
  • Care coordinated with your physician
  • Subject to eligibility, physician orders, and payer approval
Home Health AideHelp with daily routines

What it is

A trained aide helps with personal care, so daily life is safer and easier.

Who it helps

Anyone who needs a hand with bathing, dressing, or moving around safely.

What's included

  • Bathing and grooming
  • Dressing and personal hygiene
  • Moving safely around the home
  • Help with meals
  • Light housekeeping related to care
Wound CareHealing wounds safely at home

What it is

Nurse visits to clean, treat, and watch a wound, with updates to your doctor.

Who it helps

People with surgical wounds, pressure sores, diabetic wounds, or wounds that are slow to heal.

What's included

  • Wound assessment and treatment
  • Dressing changes
  • Watching for signs of infection
  • Teaching on wound care
  • Updates to your physician
Infusion NursingIV medication at home

What it is

A nurse gives IV medications and cares for IV lines safely at home.

Who it helps

Patients prescribed IV therapy who'd rather be treated at home than at a clinic.

What's included

  • IV medication given by a nurse
  • IV line care
  • Monitoring during treatment
  • Coordination with your doctor and pharmacy
Patient and Caregiver EducationConfidence to manage care at home

What it is

Simple, practical teaching so you and your family know what to do between visits.

Who it helps

Patients and family caregivers adjusting to a new diagnosis, medication, or routine.

What's included

  • Understanding your condition
  • Managing medications
  • Warning signs to watch for
  • Safe care techniques for family
Care CoordinationEveryone on the same page

What it is

We keep your doctors, specialists, and family connected and informed.

Who it helps

Anyone with several doctors or a recent move from hospital to home.

What's included

  • Communication with your physicians
  • Smooth hospital-to-home transitions
  • Updates for your family
  • Coordination with other providers

Services are provided within our licensed scope and follow physician orders, eligibility, and payer requirements. Ashram will begin providing services upon AHCA licensure.
