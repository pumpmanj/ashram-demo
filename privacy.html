Information we collect

When you contact us through this website, we collect the information you choose to share, such as your name, email address, phone number, county, and the type of care or role you're interested in.

How we use it

  • To respond to your questions and requests
  • To contact you about care, referrals, or employment you asked about
  • To improve our website and services

Medical information

Please do not send medical or health details through website forms. We'll discuss health information with you directly and protect it as required by law, including HIPAA once we are providing care.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate this website (such as our form provider), or when required by law.
